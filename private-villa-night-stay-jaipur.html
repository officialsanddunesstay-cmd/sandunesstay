Night Stay FAQs

Can we book a private villa for just one night near Jaipur? +
Yes. Single night bookings available. Minimum 5 guests required. WhatsApp to confirm availability: +91-77373-45245.
Is the pool available at night? +
Yes. The private pool has no "pool hours" — it's exclusively yours for your entire stay, day and night.
Is there a curfew or a time we need to stop music? +
There's no imposed curfew inside the estate. Reasonable consideration for neighbours is expected, but the property sits apart from dense residential areas.
Can we do a BBQ or use the outdoor kitchen at night? +
Yes. The BBQ area, fireplace and outdoor kitchen are available whenever you want through the night, and outside cooks are welcome.
How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur Airport for a late arrival? +
About 20km, roughly 25 minutes via NH48 from Jaipur (Sanganer) Airport.
What is the minimum group size for a one-night stay? +
Minimum booking is 5 guests. A single night is fine as long as that minimum is met — confirm exact numbers on WhatsApp.
Is late check-in possible? +
Yes, late check-in can be arranged. Standard check-in is 12PM and check-out is 11AM; timing for a night-stay plan can be discussed on WhatsApp.
Can outside DJs or a sound system be brought in for the night? +
Yes. Outside vendors including DJs are welcome, and the property also has its own sound system available.
Is parking available for a group arriving at night? +
Yes, free on-site parking is available for all guest vehicles.
How many rooms and how many guests can stay overnight? +
The estate has 9 rooms (6 Deluxe + 3 Super Deluxe) and comfortably sleeps 8-15 guests overnight.
Is this good for a couple's one-night getaway? +
Yes — many guests book a single night as a couples' escape, subject to the 5-guest minimum policy. Ask on WhatsApp about combining with another small group if needed.
What's included in a one-night stay? +
Access to the private pool, three garden areas, party hall, outdoor fireplace, BBQ area, sun terrace and full kitchen, along with free WiFi, AC and parking.
Can we swim at midnight? +
Yes, the pool is 100% private and exclusive to your group for the full duration of the stay, so a midnight swim is entirely possible.
Where exactly is Sand Dunes Stay located? +
📍 Rampura, Unti, Bagru, Jaipur 303007 — Bagru, Jaipur (NOT Jaisalmer — 575km away), directly on the NH48 (Ajmer Road) corridor, about 25km from Jaipur city centre.
How do we book a one-night stay? +
Message the property directly on WhatsApp at +91-77373-45245 with your dates and group size, or check availability through the direct website or listed OTA partners.
