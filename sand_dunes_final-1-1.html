Getting Here

How Far Is Sand Dunes Stay?

Sand Dunes Stay is one of the most conveniently located luxury villas near Jaipur — close to the airport, easy to reach from the city, and tucked into the peaceful desert landscape around Bagru, Mahla, and Bhagrota.

Jaipur International Airport

~20 km

Approx. 25–35 min drive. Sand Dunes Stay is one of the closest luxury villas to Jaipur Airport — perfect for fly-in stays.

Jaipur City Centre

~25 km

From central Jaipur (MI Road / Sindhi Camp), reach us in about 35–45 minutes via the Ajmer Road or Bagru bypass route.

Bagru Town

~5 km

Bagru — known for its traditional block-printing heritage — is just 5 km away. The nearest major town for supplies and services.

Mahla Village

~3 km

Mahla is a nearby village just a few minutes from our estate, forming part of the peaceful rural corridor surrounding Sand Dunes Stay.

Bhagrota Village

~4 km

Bhagrota is another nearby settlement in the Bagru belt. The surrounding landscape of Mahla, Bhagrota, and Unti gives Sand Dunes Stay its true desert character.

Jaipur Railway Station

~28 km

From Jaipur Junction railway station, the drive to Sand Dunes Stay takes approximately 40–50 minutes via NH-48 toward Bagru.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers about Sand Dunes Stay — our location, amenities, booking options, and what to expect.

Where is Sand Dunes Stay located?
Sand Dunes Stay is located at Rampura, Unti, Bagru, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It sits approximately 25 km from Jaipur City Centre and just ~20 km from Jaipur International Airport, making it one of the most accessible luxury villas near the airport in Rajasthan. The nearest landmark town is Bagru, a well-known block-printing town on the outskirts of Jaipur.
How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur Airport?
Sand Dunes Stay is approximately 20 km from Jaipur International Airport (Sanganer Airport). The drive typically takes 25–35 minutes depending on traffic. This makes it ideal for guests flying into Jaipur who want a private luxury villa stay without the noise and crowds of the city.
What amenities does Sand Dunes Stay offer?
Sand Dunes Stay is a 6,000 sq. metre luxury villa near Bagru, Jaipur with the following amenities:
  • 9 Private Rooms — 6 Deluxe + 3 Super Deluxe
  • Private Swimming Pool — exclusively for your group, no shared timing
  • 3 Garden Areas — open, manicured spaces for gatherings and celebrations
  • Dedicated Party Hall — ideal for events, performances, and dining setups
  • Full Kitchen — gas, utensils, and workspace for your own caterer or cook
  • Private Parking — on-site, no street parking required
  • 100% Privacy — no other guests on the property during your stay
Can I book the entire villa for a wedding or event?
Yes. Sand Dunes Stay offers a full property takeover where your group gets exclusive access to all 9 rooms, the private pool, all 3 gardens, and the party hall — with no other guests on the estate. This setup is popular for destination weddings, sangeet nights, birthday parties, anniversary celebrations, and corporate retreats near Jaipur. The desert landscape and open-sky setting make every event uniquely memorable.
Can I book individual rooms, or must I take the whole property?
Both options are available. You can book individual rooms on a per-night basis — ideal for smaller groups — or opt for the full property takeover for complete privacy. The estate has 6 Deluxe rooms and 3 Super Deluxe rooms. Note: shared common areas may apply when individual rooms are booked and other guests are also present.
Is Sand Dunes Stay a desert property? What's the landscape like?
Sand Dunes Stay is set in the semi-arid Rajasthan landscape on the outskirts of Jaipur, near the villages of Mahla, Bhagrota, and Unti in the Bagru belt. The estate offers open desert skies, natural terrain, and total quiet — a complete escape from the city. Despite being a true desert-edge retreat, it is only 20 km from Jaipur Airport and easily accessible from Jaipur City.
How do I reach Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur?
The most common route is via NH-48 (Ajmer Road), turning toward Bagru and then following directions to Rampura, Unti village. Exact driving instructions and GPS coordinates are shared after booking confirmation. You can also use our Google Maps pin to navigate directly.
