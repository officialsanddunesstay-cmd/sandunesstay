Got Questions?

Private Pool Villa — FAQs

Everything you want to know about the pool, the villa, and your stay.

Is the pool actually private — or shared with other guests?+
100% private. When you book the villa, the pool is yours alone. No other group, no resort guests, no strangers. What "private pool villa" means at Sand Dunes Stay is that the entire property — including the pool — is booked exclusively for your group. Not just a time slot.
What are the pool timings? Can we swim at night?+
There are no fixed pool timings. As a private villa guest, you have access to the pool 24 hours a day throughout your stay. Night swims, late-evening dips, early morning laps — all completely fine. No check-in or check-out required for the pool.
How large is the pool? Is it suitable for swimming laps?+
The pool is well-sized for both leisure and lap swimming. It comfortably accommodates a group for pool parties, floating, and games. For exact dimensions, please WhatsApp us and we'll send current specs along with photos.
Can we host a pool party at the villa?+
Yes, and it's one of the most popular uses. Birthdays, bachelorettes, anniversaries, office parties — the pool area is perfect for celebrations. We recommend WhatsApping us in advance so we can help you plan the setup, catering options, and any additional arrangements.
Is the pool heated? What temperature is the water?+
Pool temperature varies by season. Rajasthan summers mean naturally warm water, while winter months may feel cooler. Please WhatsApp us close to your travel date and we'll confirm the pool conditions and any heating options available.
Are there additional charges for pool use?+
No. Pool access is fully included in your villa booking. There are no extra charges for using the pool, no per-hour fees, and no deposit. The price you see covers your group for the entire stay, pool included.
Is the pool safe for children?+
The pool has depth markings and a slip-resistant deck. Children must be supervised by adults at all times near the pool. If you'd like floatation aids arranged in advance, let us know via WhatsApp and we can help organise them.
Can we do a pre-wedding shoot or photography at the pool?+
Absolutely. The private pool and villa grounds are ideal for pre-wedding shoots, couple photography, maternity shoots, and content creation. No crowds, no background clutter, no booking window — just the pool, your group, and your photographer. Enquire via WhatsApp to book a photography-specific session.
How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur city and the airport?+
The villa is located in Bagru, approximately 35–45 minutes from central Jaipur and around 20–25 minutes from Jaipur International Airport. It's on a major highway, making it very easy to reach. Cab and taxi services from Jaipur city or the airport are readily available.
How many people does the pool villa accommodate?+
We offer two villa options — a 3-bedroom and a 5-bedroom. The 3BR villa suits groups of 5–10 guests, while the 5BR is ideal for 10–15+ guests. Both include the same private pool access. For larger events, WhatsApp us to discuss a custom arrangement.
Can we have food and drinks by the pool?+
Yes — one of the joys of a private pool villa. You can dine, drink, and celebrate poolside freely. The villa has a self-cooking facility and we can also arrange catering for larger groups. Alcohol is permitted on the private premises. Please WhatsApp us for any specific catering or bar setup requests.
Is the pool area covered or open-air?+
The pool is open-air, with Rajasthan's wide-open sky above — spectacular for sunsets, stargazing at night, and golden hour photography. The surrounding deck area provides space for lounging, dining, and relaxing outside the water.
What's the check-in and check-out time for the villa?+
Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available depending on availability — please WhatsApp us in advance to arrange. Pool access is included right from check-in.
Can we bring our own music system or DJ for the pool party?+
Yes, you're welcome to bring music and set up your own audio by the pool. For DJ setups or larger sound systems, please let us know in advance so we can coordinate accordingly. Being a private property, you won't disturb any other guests.
Is the villa pet-friendly?+
Please WhatsApp us directly to confirm pet policy at the time of booking, as this may vary. We'll be happy to discuss your specific situation and see how we can accommodate you.
Is advance booking required or can we do a walk-in?+
Advance booking is strongly recommended. The villa is a private property and is booked exclusively for one group at a time — which means availability is limited. Weekends and holidays tend to fill up quickly. Book at least a week ahead, or earlier for peak season dates.
Do you offer a corporate or group discount for large bookings?+
Yes, for multi-night stays, large groups, or repeat bookings we can discuss custom pricing. WhatsApp us with your group size, dates, and any specific requirements and we'll send you a tailored quote.
Is Sand Dunes Stay near Jaisalmer?+
No — Sand Dunes Stay is located in Bagru, near Jaipur (the Pink City), not near Jaisalmer. The address is in Rajasthan, approximately 35–45 minutes from central Jaipur. We're an ideal stopover or base if you're exploring the Jaipur region, but we're not a Jaisalmer property.
