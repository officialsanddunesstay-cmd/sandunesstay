FAQs

Farmhouse in Jaipur — Common Questions

What exactly is a "farmhouse" in Jaipur — is it different from a villa?
In Indian parlance, a "farmhouse" refers to a large private estate outside the city — usually with sprawling grounds, a pool, and full exclusivity. Sand Dunes Stay is exactly this: a farmhouse-style luxury villa estate in Bagru, Jaipur. You get the grandeur of a farmhouse with the comfort of a luxury villa — the best of both.
How large is the farmhouse? What are the actual dimensions?
The estate covers approximately 6,000 square metres (about 1.5 acres). This includes the main villa building, private pool, party hall, open lawns, garden spaces, and ample parking. It is genuinely large — not a studio or apartment that calls itself a "villa."
How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur city?
We are located in Bagru, approximately 35–45 minutes from Jaipur city centre (Pink City / Hawa Mahal area) depending on traffic. We are also conveniently located near Manipal University Jaipur — ideal for families visiting students. It's the perfect distance — far enough to feel like a real getaway, close enough to be convenient.
Is the entire farmhouse private, or are there other guests?
100% private. When you book Sand Dunes Stay, you get exclusive access to the entire estate — all bedrooms, the pool, party hall, gardens, and common areas. There are no other guests on the property during your stay. This is complete, genuine exclusivity.
What is the minimum and maximum capacity for a farmhouse booking?
We comfortably accommodate 5 to 15 guests for overnight stays. For day events and parties (without overnight stay), we can host up to 50+ guests in our party hall and outdoor areas. Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss your specific group size and requirements.
How many bedrooms are available at the farmhouse?
Sand Dunes Stay offers up to 5 fully furnished, en-suite bedrooms — including a 3-bedroom configuration and a 5-bedroom configuration available on Airbnb. All rooms have attached bathrooms, quality bedding, and are air-conditioned. See our Rooms page for details.
Is the swimming pool private? Can we use it at night?
Yes — the swimming pool is entirely private and reserved exclusively for your group. You can use it throughout your stay, including evenings. The pool area is lit for evening use. We do have standard safety guidelines in place, and night swimming is subject to reasonable responsibility from guests.
What is the price for farmhouse rent in Jaipur at Sand Dunes Stay?
Pricing starts from ₹15,000 per night and varies based on guest count, dates, season, and any add-on services (DJ, decoration, catering). We don't publish fixed rate charts because every booking is personalised. The quickest way to get exact pricing is to WhatsApp us with your dates and group size.
Can we cook our own food at the farmhouse?
Yes — Sand Dunes Stay has a fully equipped modern kitchen available for self-cooking. You're welcome to bring groceries and cook for your group. We also offer catering coordination if you prefer cooked meals delivered or prepared on-site. See our Self-Cooking Villa page for details.
Is this farmhouse suitable for a pool party in Jaipur?
Absolutely — pool parties are one of our most popular occasions. Our private pool, outdoor lawns, party hall, and DJ support make Sand Dunes Stay one of the best pool party venues in Jaipur. We can arrange lighting, music, decoration, and catering for a complete pool party experience. Visit our Pool Party page for details.
Can we organise a birthday party at the farmhouse?
Yes — birthday celebrations are very popular here. Our party hall, pool, open lawns, and decoration support make for an unforgettable birthday. We have hosted birthday parties for children, young adults, and milestone birthdays (30th, 50th, etc.) with equal success. Visit our Birthday Party page to learn more.
Is Sand Dunes Stay suitable for a pre-wedding shoot?
Very much so. Our golden Rajasthan landscape, private pool, manicured gardens, and architectural details create stunning backdrops for pre-wedding photography. Many professional photographers choose Sand Dunes Stay specifically for the variety of settings available on one property. See our Pre-Wedding Shoot page.
Do you allow DJ and music at the farmhouse?
Yes — DJ nights, live music, and sound systems are welcome at Sand Dunes Stay. We are located away from densely populated areas, which means your event can genuinely turn up the volume without disturbing neighbours. We work with trusted DJ vendors and can coordinate the setup for you.
Is there parking available at the farmhouse?
Yes — ample parking is available on-site for multiple cars and SUVs. If you're travelling in a large group across multiple vehicles, you can comfortably park on the property. Buses and larger vehicles can also be accommodated with prior notice.
How do I book the farmhouse? What is the process?
The easiest way is to WhatsApp us with your dates, guest count, and occasion. We'll confirm availability, share pricing, and guide you through the booking. A partial advance secures your booking. You can also book directly via Airbnb (3 BR) or Airbnb (5 BR).
Is Sand Dunes Stay pet-friendly?
We understand how important furry family members are! Please mention pets at the time of enquiry and we'll confirm based on current availability and any conditions. We evaluate on a case-by-case basis with the aim of accommodating your whole family.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and checkout is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late checkout can be arranged based on availability — please ask when booking. For day-use bookings (party or event without overnight stay), timings are agreed in advance based on your event schedule.
