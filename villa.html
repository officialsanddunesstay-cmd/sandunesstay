Villa with Pool

What Is Included in the Villa Booking?

The Villa experience at Sand Dunes Stay gives your group exclusive access to 5 beautifully furnished private rooms and a dedicated private swimming pool — all within a sprawling private estate at Rampura Unti Bagru, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

This is the ideal booking for groups of up to 10 who want a private luxury pool villa experience without taking over the entire estate. Your five rooms, your pool, your garden and your kitchen — all reserved exclusively for your group.

The Villa is part of the larger Sand Dunes Stay estate, spread across 6,000 sq.m. of private grounds. It includes access to one of the estate's most beautiful garden areas, the full kitchen and cafeteria, and daily housekeeping service.
