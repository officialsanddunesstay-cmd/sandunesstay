About the Rooms

Rooms & Accommodation at Sand Dunes Stay

8 private rooms across a 6,000 sqm estate — comfortable, air-conditioned, and built for groups who want the whole place to themselves.

Sand Dunes Stay, Bagru Jaipur mein 8 rooms available hain — 5 individually photographed rooms main villa mein (Room 101 se Room 105 tak), aur 3 rooms Sand Villa 2 mein, jo estate ke gardens mein set hai. Har room mein air conditioning, free high-speed WiFi, LED TV, aur hot water ke saath attached bathroom milta hai.

Rooms share nahi hote kisi stranger ke saath — jab aap book karte hain, poora group apna private space le leta hai, aur private pool access har booking ke saath included hota hai. Chahe aap 5 guests ke saath chhoti family trip plan kar rahe hon, ya 15 guests ke saath poori property book karna chahte hon, Sand Dunes Stay ke rooms flexible stay options dete hain.

8 se 15 guests overnight stay kar sakte hain, aur minimum booking 5 guests ki hai. Events aur day celebrations ke liye, estate 100 guests tak accommodate kar sakta hai, party hall aur open gardens ke saath.

FAQs

Rooms & Accommodation — FAQs

Rooms, amenities, pricing aur booking ke baare mein sabse zyada puche jaane waale sawaal.

How many rooms does Sand Dunes Stay have? +
Sand Dunes Stay has 8 rooms in total — 5 individually photographed rooms (Room 101 to 105) in the main villa, plus 3 rooms in Sand Villa 2, set in the gardens. All rooms are air-conditioned and part of the fully private 6,000 sqm estate in Bagru, Jaipur.
What is Sand Villa 2? +
Sand Villa 2 is a separate garden villa on the same estate, with 3 rooms offering the same facilities as the rest of the property — AC, WiFi, LED TV, and hot water.
What is included in each room? +
Every room includes air conditioning, free high-speed WiFi, an LED TV, an attached bathroom with hot water, and comfortable modern furnishings.
Can I book individual rooms or only the full property? +
You can book a few rooms for a smaller stay, or the entire estate — all 8 rooms, the pool, gardens, party hall and kitchen — for complete exclusivity. WhatsApp +91-77373-45245 with your group size for the best option.
Are all rooms air-conditioned? +
Yes. All 8 rooms — across the main villa and Sand Villa 2 — are fully air-conditioned.
Is hot water available in rooms? +
Yes. Hot water is available in all attached bathrooms across all 8 rooms.
Is WiFi available in rooms? +
Yes. Free high-speed WiFi is available in all rooms and across the outdoor areas of the estate.
What is the maximum overnight guest capacity? +
Sand Dunes Stay can accommodate up to 15 guests overnight. For events and day celebrations, the estate can host up to 100 guests.
What is the minimum number of guests to book? +
The minimum booking is for 5 guests. WhatsApp +91-77373-45245 to confirm availability for your group size.
Is private pool access included with room booking? +
Yes. The private pool is 100% exclusive and included with every booking — never shared with other guests.
Are there attached bathrooms in all rooms? +
Yes. All 8 rooms — in the main villa and Sand Villa 2 — have private, attached bathrooms with hot water.
What is the room price per night? +
Rooms start from ₹15,000 per night for the group. Exact pricing depends on your group size and stay dates. WhatsApp +91-77373-45245 for a personalised quote.
What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Check-in is at 12:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. For early check-in or late check-out, please WhatsApp +91-77373-45245 to check availability.
Can I see room photos before booking? +
Yes. The photo gallery above on this page shows the rooms, pool, and estate. You can also view more on Instagram: @sand_dunes_stay.
How do I book rooms at Sand Dunes Stay? +
The easiest way is to WhatsApp +91-77373-45245 directly. You can also book via sanddunesstay.com, Airbnb, Booking.com, or Agoda. Direct booking usually gets the best price.
