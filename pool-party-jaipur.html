Planning Guide

Pool Party Setup Guide — Decorations, Music & Food

Use this as your checklist. Our team can connect you to vendors for each category — just WhatsApp us.

🌊 Floating Pool Decor

Inflatable flamingos, flower rings, and LED floaties create Instagram-ready visuals. Order 3–4 days ahead from Jaipur party stores.

🎈 Balloon Arches

A poolside balloon arch in your theme colours makes the perfect entry or photo-op. Takes 1–2 hrs to set up — plan accordingly.

💡 Fairy Lights & Lanterns

String lights around the pool edge and garden area look magical after sunset. Works especially well for evening parties in March–October.

🌸 Flower Setups

Floating marigolds, rose petals on water, or a floral backdrop wall. Jaipur flower markets are very affordable — local florists do pool setups.

🪑 Lounge Furniture

Bean bags, pool-edge loungers, and cabana-style seating rentals available. Ask us to connect you with local event rental vendors.

🎂 Cake Table Setup

Birthday or celebration pool parties benefit from a dedicated dessert table near the pool edge with signage and lighting.

🎛️ Outside DJ — Fully Allowed

Bring your own DJ or we can recommend experienced pool-party DJs who know how to read a crowd and manage outdoor acoustics.

🔊 Sound System

Waterproof outdoor speakers can be arranged. DJ setup area is located poolside with power access. Confirm power requirements when booking.

🎤 Emcee / Host

For birthday pool parties with games and activities, an emcee keeps energy high. We can recommend bilingual (Hindi/English) hosts.

🎶 Playlist Tips

Pool parties work best with high-energy Bollywood, EDM, or remixes in the afternoon; switch to chill lofi or acoustic for evening dining sections.

🔥 BBQ & Grill

Our BBQ setup is great for groups. Skewers, corn, paneer tikka, and chicken work beautifully for poolside snacking between dips.

🍳 Self-Cooking Option

Use our fully-equipped kitchen. Great for intimate groups who prefer home-cooked food or want to keep the experience personal.

🍱 Caterer Bring-In

Bring your preferred caterer. We allow outside catering — just inform us while booking so we can coordinate kitchen and serving area logistics.

🥤 Mocktail & Drinks Bar

Set up a DIY drinks station with fresh juices, nimbu paani, aam panna, and mocktails. Budget ₹150–250/head for a self-serve setup.

🍭 Pool Snack Stations

Chips, nachos, fruit platters, and cold chaats near the pool edge — easy to grab between swims without needing full service.

🎂 Birthday Cakes

Jaipur has excellent custom cake bakers. Order 3–5 days ahead. We provide a dedicated table area and can arrange candle/sparkler moments.

🌴 Tropical / Hawaii

Leis, tiki torches, tropical prints, and a fruit platter centrepiece. Works especially well for summer afternoons (May–July).

🌙 Bollywood Night Pool

Fairy lights, Bollywood playlist, ethnic decor with marigolds and diyas. A crowd-pleaser for birthday parties with mixed age groups.

🦋 Neon & Glow

UV lights, neon face paint, and glow sticks transform the pool area after dark. Perfect for young adult groups aged 18–30.

🎀 Pastel & Boho

Macramé hangings, soft florals, and a pastel colour palette — popular for women's birthday parties and bridal pool celebrations.

🏆 Sports & Pool Games

Pool volleyball, float races, and dive competitions. Our pool is sized well for group pool games — great for college group outings.

🌅 Sunset Rooftop-Pool Combo

Start at the pool, shift to rooftop at golden hour for sunset photos, then return to poolside for an evening party. A favourite itinerary.

💡 Booking Tips by Season

  • 🌞 April–June: Book 2–4 weeks ahead. These are the hottest months — perfect pool weather, highest demand.
  • 🌧️ July–August: Monsoon brings cooler temps — evening pool parties are magical. Lower demand means better availability.
  • 🍂 September–October: Post-monsoon freshness. Great for daytime parties and weekend group stays.
  • ❄️ November–February: Pool is still available but water can be cool. Heated options and indoor events work better.
