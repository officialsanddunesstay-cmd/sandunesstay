FAQ

Kitty Party Venue — Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a good kitty party venue near Jaipur? +
Yes. Sand Dunes Stay in Bagru is Jaipur's finest private villa kitty party venue — with an exclusive pool, lush garden, indoor party hall, and day-booking option. Just 25km / 30 minutes from Jaipur city centre on NH48.
Can we do a pool kitty party at Sand Dunes Stay? +
Absolutely. The private pool is 100% exclusive to your group — no other guests, no strangers sharing it. It's the perfect setting for a pool kitty party that's safe, fun, and completely private.
Do you allow day use for kitty parties? +
Yes. Day bookings are available for kitty parties without an overnight stay. Timings, pricing, and availability — WhatsApp us at +91-77373-45245 for a quick quote.
Can we bring our own caterer or cook for the kitty party? +
Yes, completely. Outside caterers, home cooks, dabbawallas, and chefs are all welcome. Sand Dunes Stay has a full kitchen available for your team's use.
How many ladies can attend a kitty party at Sand Dunes Stay? +
The villa comfortably hosts up to 15 ladies for an indoor stay, and up to 100 guests in the outdoor garden areas. Perfect for both intimate and large kitty party groups.
Is the venue completely private — ladies only? +
Yes. When you book Sand Dunes Stay, the entire 6,000 sq.m property is exclusively yours. No shared spaces, no other guests, no outsiders — complete privacy for your group.
Is parking available for all guests? +
Yes. Free, ample parking is available on the property for all guests. No parking issues, no hassle.
How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur city? +
Sand Dunes Stay is 25km from Jaipur city centre — approximately 30 minutes by car via NH48 (Ajmer Road). It's also 20km / 25 minutes from Jaipur International Airport (Sanganer). The property is in Bagru, Jaipur — not Jaisalmer (which is 575km away).
Can we bring a DJ or decorator for our kitty party theme? +
Yes. Outside DJs, decorators, photographers, and event vendors are all welcome at Sand Dunes Stay. We have no in-house vendor restrictions — bring whoever makes your kitty party special.
What is the minimum group size for a kitty party booking? +
The minimum booking is for 5 guests. For kitty parties, WhatsApp us with your group size and we'll arrange the right package for you.
Can we do a themed photoshoot at the villa during our kitty party? +
Absolutely. The private pool, garden areas, sun terrace, and villa interiors provide stunning backdrops for themed photoshoots. Many groups combine their kitty party with a professional or group photoshoot session.
What kitty party formats are popular at Sand Dunes Stay? +
Pool kitty, garden brunch kitty, BBQ evening kitty, photoshoot kitty, theme party kitty, and birthday-combo kitty are all popular. Every format can be customised to your group's preferences and timing.
Is WiFi and music system available? +
Yes. Free high-speed WiFi and a professional sound system are included in every booking at Sand Dunes Stay. Bring your kitty party playlist and set the mood.
How do I check availability and book a kitty party? +
WhatsApp is the fastest way: +91-77373-45245. Share your preferred date, group size, and format (day-use or overnight) for a quick response and personalised quote. You can also email: officialsanddunestay@gmail.com
What is the pricing for a kitty party at Sand Dunes Stay? +
Pricing depends on group size, duration, and whether it's day-use or overnight. Starting rates from ₹15,000. WhatsApp for a personalised quote: +91-77373-45245. No pricing tables — we tailor every quote to your group.
