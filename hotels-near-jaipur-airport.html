Airport to Sand Dunes Stay — 20 Minutes via NH48

NH48 is one of the most well-maintained highways in Rajasthan. The drive from Jaipur Airport is smooth, straight, and toll-free within this stretch.

✈ Jaipur International Airport → Sand Dunes Stay, Bagru

  1. 1
    Exit Jaipur Airport (Sanganer) Take the main exit from the airport terminal. Head towards the NH48 / Delhi–Mumbai Expressway service road. Follow signs for Ajmer / Kishangarh direction.
  2. 2
    Merge onto NH48 heading towards Ajmer Once on NH48, drive straight. This is a 4-lane national highway — smooth and well-lit. No city traffic at this stretch. Distance from airport to Bagru exit: ~17 km.
  3. 3
    Take the Bagru Exit from NH48 Watch for the Bagru / Bhambori signboard on NH48. Take the exit and head into Bagru town. This is roughly 15–18 minutes from the airport.
  4. 4
    Follow Google Maps to Sand Dunes Stay Once in Bagru, navigate using Google Maps to Sand Dunes Stay. The property is clearly marked on Google Business Profile. The final stretch takes 3–5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Arrive — Total Drive Time: ~20–25 minutes You've arrived at your private villa. WhatsApp us before landing — we'll have everything ready for your arrival.
