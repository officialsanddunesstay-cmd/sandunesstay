Frequently Asked Questions

Delhi Group Trip — FAQs

Is there a group villa on the Delhi-Jaipur highway?

Yes. Sand Dunes Stay in Bagru is directly on NH48, the Delhi-Jaipur highway — 280km from Delhi (~5hrs drive). It's a private pool estate for 8-15 guests with 9 rooms on 1.5 acres.

Can a group of friends from Delhi book Sand Dunes Stay for a weekend?

Absolutely. Drive Friday evening, arrive by midnight, enjoy Saturday at the private pool and Jaipur sightseeing, then checkout Sunday morning and drive back — the classic Delhi weekend formula.

How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Delhi?

Sand Dunes Stay is approximately 280km from Delhi via NH48 — about 4.5 to 5 hours depending on traffic. From Gurgaon it's around 240km (~4 hrs).

Which highway connects Delhi and Sand Dunes Stay?

NH48 (also called the Delhi-Jaipur Highway or Ajmer Road) connects Delhi directly to Bagru. Sand Dunes Stay is just off the NH48 Bagru exit, making it one of the most accessible villas on this route.

What is the route from Delhi to Sand Dunes Stay?

Delhi → NH48 → Gurgaon → Manesar → Shahjahanpur → Bhiwadi → Kotputli → Jaipur → Bagru exit → Sand Dunes Stay. Total: approximately 280km, ~5hrs.

How many guests can Sand Dunes Stay accommodate from Delhi?

Sand Dunes Stay accommodates 8 to 15 overnight guests (minimum 5) across 9 rooms — 6 Deluxe and 3 Super Deluxe. For day events the property can host up to 100 guests outdoors.

What is the minimum booking size for a group from Delhi?

The minimum booking is for 5 guests. For a comfortable group stay from Delhi, most groups book for 8-12 guests to maximize the experience across the estate.

How much does it cost to book Sand Dunes Stay for a Delhi group trip?

Pricing starts from ₹15,000 per night. Split across 8-15 people, the per-person cost is very affordable. WhatsApp for exact rates based on your group size and dates.

Is Sand Dunes Stay pool private or shared with other guests?

The pool is 100% private. When you book Sand Dunes Stay, the entire villa and pool is exclusively yours — no other guests, no shared spaces, no time restrictions.

Can we do Jaipur sightseeing from Sand Dunes Stay during a Delhi trip?

Yes. Jaipur City Centre is just 25km (~30min) from the villa. Hawa Mahal, Amer Fort, Nahargarh, and the old city markets are all easily reachable for a day-trip drive from the estate.

Can we bring a DJ and do a pool party at Sand Dunes Stay?

Yes. Outside vendors — DJ, decor, caterers — are all welcome. The property has a party hall, outdoor garden spaces, fireplace, and BBQ. Just inform us in advance when booking.

What is check-in and check-out time?

Check-in is from 12 PM. Check-out is by 11 AM. If you're driving from Delhi and arriving late at night, coordinate with us via WhatsApp and we'll make sure everything is ready for you.

Is there parking at Sand Dunes Stay for a large group driving from Delhi?

Yes, free parking is available on the property. There's ample space for multiple vehicles — no parking hassle after a long highway drive from Delhi.

Is Sand Dunes Stay near Jaisalmer?

📍 Bagru, Jaipur (NOT Jaisalmer — 575km away). Sand Dunes Stay is in Bagru, Jaipur — right on the Delhi-Jaipur highway NH48. It's perfectly located for Delhi groups who want a weekend at a private villa with a pool near Jaipur.
