What Is Ajmer Road / NH48?

Understanding the route — and why Sand Dunes Stay is the standout address on this corridor.

🛣️ NH48 — Delhi to Ahmedabad

National Highway 48 is one of India's most traveled arterial roads, connecting Delhi → Jaipur → Ajmer → Ahmedabad. In Jaipur, this stretch is commonly known as Ajmer Road — passing through Sanganer, Bagru, and beyond. Thousands of travelers, corporate delegations and road-trip groups traverse this corridor daily.
