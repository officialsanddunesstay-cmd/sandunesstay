Frequently Asked Questions

Villa Rental — Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before booking your villa rental in Jaipur.

What is the minimum duration to rent the villa? +

Minimum overnight rental is one night (check-in 2 PM, check-out 11 AM next day). For day-use events we offer 6-hour or 8-hour slots — no overnight stay required. WhatsApp us for slot availability on your preferred date.

What is the villa rental price per night in Jaipur? +

Overnight rental starts from ₹15,000/night for the 3-bedroom option (up to 10 guests) and from ₹20,000/night for the full 5-bedroom estate (up to 15 guests). Final price depends on guest count and date. WhatsApp for an exact quote — we respond within the hour.

Can I rent the villa for just one day without staying overnight? +

Yes. We offer day-use rentals (6-hour or 8-hour slots) for pool parties, birthday gatherings, pre-wedding shoots, corporate events and more. Starting from ₹12,000 per slot. Check availability on WhatsApp.

How much advance / deposit is required to confirm a booking? +

A refundable security deposit and a partial advance (typically 25–30% of the rental amount) are collected to confirm the booking. The balance is paid before check-in. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and online payment. No surprise charges.

What is the cancellation policy for villa rentals? +

Cancellations made 7+ days before check-in receive a full advance refund. Cancellations within 7 days are subject to a partial retention (typically 50% of advance). Last-minute cancellations (within 48 hours) may forfeit the advance. We always try to accommodate genuine emergencies — just WhatsApp us and we'll work something out.

Is the pool included in all rental options? +

Yes. Access to the private pool is included in all rental types — overnight stays, weekend rentals, and day-use event slots. The pool is cleaned before every check-in / event. You never share it with another group.

Can I cook my own food at the villa? +

Absolutely. The villa has a fully equipped kitchen — gas hobs, microwave, utensils, and crockery. You're welcome to self-cook. We can also arrange catering (local Rajasthani, North Indian, and continental menus) — just let us know at the time of booking.

How many guests can the villa accommodate? +

The 3-bedroom option comfortably fits up to 10 guests for an overnight stay. The full 5-bedroom estate accommodates up to 15 overnight guests. For day events (no overnight stay), the lawn can host up to 50 guests. WhatsApp us for larger requirements.

Is there parking available at the rental villa? +

Yes, free on-property parking is included for all rental guests. We can accommodate multiple cars and a mini-bus. No external parking hassles.

Can I host a birthday party or event during the rental? +

Yes, that's one of our most popular use cases. You can host birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, and small celebrations during any rental. We allow décor, catering, and music (within reasonable hours). External decorators and caterers are welcome on the property.

Is the villa pet-friendly? +

We welcome well-behaved pets. Please let us know at the time of booking so we can make appropriate arrangements. A small additional cleaning charge may apply.

What is the check-in and check-out time? +

Standard check-in is from 2 PM and check-out by 11 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can sometimes be arranged depending on availability — please request this at the time of booking. No charges for requests made in advance.

Is the villa suitable for a pre-wedding shoot rental? +

Very much so. The villa's garden, pool, and heritage architecture make it one of the best pre-wedding shoot locations near Jaipur. We offer both full-day and half-day shoot rentals. External photographers and cinematographers are welcome.

Are there any additional charges beyond the rental rate? +

The rental price covers pool access, all bedrooms, kitchen, garden, Wi-Fi, parking, and utilities. Optional add-ons (catering, décor, bonfire setup) are charged separately at transparent rates. We quote everything upfront — no hidden costs.

How far is the villa from Jaipur city centre? +

The villa is located in Bagru, approximately 35 km from Jaipur city centre and Jaipur International Airport — about 35–45 minutes by car. It's well connected via the Ajmer Road. Getting directions is easy via the Google Maps link we share at booking.

Can I book the villa for a corporate team outing? +

Yes. We regularly host corporate retreats, team offsites, and leadership getaways. The villa offers a quiet, distraction-free environment with space for indoor discussions and outdoor activities. We can arrange projector, catering, and team activity coordination. WhatsApp us with your group size and requirements.

Can I book via Airbnb or Booking.com instead of WhatsApp? +

Yes, we're listed on Airbnb (3BR and 5BR options) and Booking.com. Links are below. However, WhatsApp bookings often get better availability and allow us to tailor the experience to your exact group. Direct bookings also skip OTA service fees.

Do you offer weekly or monthly villa rentals in Jaipur? +

Extended rentals (week-long, bi-weekly, or monthly) are available on request. We offer special rates for longer-duration stays. WhatsApp us with your dates and we'll put together a custom quote for you.
