FAQs

Farmhouse in Bagru, Jaipur — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the farmhouse — location, pool, events, vendors, pricing and booking.

Is there a farmhouse in Bagru, Jaipur? +
Yes. Sand Dunes Stay is a farmhouse-style private villa estate at Rampura, Unti, Bagru, Jaipur 303007 — a 6,000 sq. m. private estate with a private pool, party hall and three gardens. Note: it is in Bagru, Jaipur, not Jaisalmer.
Which is the best farmhouse near Jaipur Airport? +
Sand Dunes Stay in Bagru is about 20 km (roughly 25 minutes) from Jaipur International Airport at Sanganer, with direct access via NH48 (Ajmer Road).
Is there a farmhouse with a private pool near Jaipur? +
Yes. The swimming pool at Sand Dunes Stay is 100% private to your group — it is never shared with other guests during your stay.
What is the farmhouse rent near Jaipur? +
Stays at Sand Dunes Stay start from ₹15,000 per night. The rate depends on group size and dates, and event pricing differs, so WhatsApp +91 77373 45245 with your dates for an exact quote.
Can I book this farmhouse for a birthday party near Jaipur? +
Yes. There is a dedicated party hall, a private pool, an outdoor BBQ area and three gardens. Events can host up to 100 guests, and the party hall can be booked without an overnight stay.
Is a farmhouse available on Ajmer Road, Jaipur? +
Yes. Sand Dunes Stay is on the NH48 (Ajmer Road) corridor at Bagru, with direct highway access from Jaipur city, about 25 km away.
Is outside catering allowed at the farmhouse? +
Yes. A fully equipped kitchen is available for self-managed cooking, and you are welcome to bring your own caterer and decor vendors. Please tell us about outside vendors in advance.
Is an outside DJ allowed? +
A sound system is available on the property and outside DJs are welcome for events. Please inform us in advance when you enquire.
How many people can stay overnight, and how many can attend an event? +
Up to 15 guests can stay overnight (minimum booking 5 guests). Events and parties can host up to 100 guests.
How many rooms does the farmhouse have? +
The main site lists 8 rooms: 5 in the main villa (Rooms 101–105) and 3 in Sand Villa 2. All rooms are air-conditioned.
Is the farmhouse completely private? +
Yes, when you book the full property. Sand Dunes Stay then accepts no other room or hall bookings for that period — the estate, pool, gardens and kitchen are exclusively yours. Individual rooms may share common areas with other guests.
Is parking available? +
Yes. Free private on-site parking is available for multiple vehicles — no street parking needed.
How far is the farmhouse from Sanganer and Bagru town? +
About 15 km (around 20 minutes) from Sanganer town and about 5 km (around 8 minutes) from Bagru town centre.
Can I host a mehndi, ring ceremony or sangeet here? +
Yes. The gardens, outdoor areas and party hall suit mehndi, ring ceremonies, haldi, sangeet and other intimate pre-wedding functions. Florists and decorators are welcome — please inform us in advance.
Is there an outdoor fireplace? +
Yes. An outdoor fireplace and BBQ area are available for evening gatherings, especially in winter.
Is the property secure? +
Yes. The property has CCTV coverage for on-site safety monitoring.
What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Check-in is from 12 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Flexible timing may be possible on full-property bookings — ask on WhatsApp when you enquire.
How do I book the farmhouse in Bagru, Jaipur? +
Message us on WhatsApp at +91 77373 45245 or book through sanddunesstay.com. The property is also listed on Airbnb, Booking.com and Agoda; booking direct is the best way to confirm rates and availability.
