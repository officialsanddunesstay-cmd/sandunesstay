Function-by-Function — How Sand Dunes Stay Works

Mehndi, haldi, sangeet, ring ceremony — each function has its own setup requirement. Here's how the estate delivers for each.

Mehndi Function Setup

The garden at Sand Dunes Stay is an ideal mehndi setting — natural light, open air and easy access for florists and mehndi artists. Set up low seating arrangements under the garden canopy, surround with marigold décor and let the cameras roll.

  • Garden area with natural daylight — ideal for mehndi photography
  • Florists, mehndi artists and decorators freely welcome
  • Multiple seating zones across 1.5 acres
  • Poolside as a photogenic secondary space
  • Kitchen for sweets and snacks setup by your caterer
  • Restrooms in all 9 rooms — guests refresh easily

Haldi Ceremony Setup

Haldi needs open space, water access and easy cleanup — Sand Dunes Stay has all three. The open garden is perfect for haldi ceremonies, and the pool right beside it makes for the most playful, colourful haldi photographs imaginable.

  • Open outdoor garden — haldi mess handled easily
  • Pool immediately adjacent — fun haldi-in-pool moments
  • Easy post-haldi cleanup for guests
  • Hot water in all 9 rooms for post-ceremony showers
  • Fireplace area doubles as a warm backdrop for sunrise haldi
  • Caterer welcome for haldi breakfast or brunch setup

Sangeet Night Setup

Sangeet comes alive at Sand Dunes Stay after sunset. The outdoor garden, with fairy-light draping, fireplace backdrop and pool reflections, creates an atmosphere no banquet hall can replicate. Bring your DJ and dance the night away under open Rajasthan skies.

  • Full outdoor garden for open-air sangeet dance floor
  • Outdoor fireplace — stunning sangeet backdrop
  • Pool lit up at night — magical reflections for photos
  • DJ setup fully welcome — outdoor speaker placement available
  • Party hall for seated dinner or overflow space
  • LED TV + sound system on estate as backup

Ring Ceremony Setup

The ring ceremony is intimate and elegant — and the estate's combination of party hall and garden gives you both an intimate indoor option and a photogenic outdoor setting. A poolside ring ceremony with garden flowers is a memory that lasts a lifetime.

  • Party hall for intimate indoor ring ceremony setup
  • Garden area as elegant outdoor backdrop
  • Poolside — stunning ring ceremony photo location
  • All vendors — florists, decorators, caterers — welcome
  • Up to 100 guests for an outdoor combined celebration
  • Family stays overnight — full estate to yourselves

FAQ

Sangeet & Mehndi Venue — Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a sangeet venue near Jaipur?

Yes. Sand Dunes Stay in Bagru is one of the finest sangeet venues near Jaipur — a private outdoor estate for up to 100 guests, just 25km from city centre on NH48. DJ and decorator are fully welcome and the pool makes for a spectacular sangeet backdrop.

Can we host mehndi and haldi at the same venue?

Absolutely. Sand Dunes Stay has multiple outdoor garden areas plus a party hall, making it perfect for back-to-back mehndi, haldi and sangeet functions over one or two days — all in one private estate.

How far is Sand Dunes Stay from Jaipur city?

Sand Dunes Stay is located in Bagru — approximately 25km / 30 minutes from Jaipur city centre and 20km from Jaipur Airport via NH48 (Ajmer Road). Easy access from all parts of the city.

How many guests can attend a sangeet or mehndi here?

The outdoor garden at Sand Dunes Stay accommodates up to 100 guests for functions like sangeet, mehndi and haldi. The party hall can be used additionally for catering, seated dinner or indoor backup space.

Can we bring our own DJ for sangeet?

Yes. Sand Dunes Stay fully welcomes outside DJ setups for sangeet nights. There is ample outdoor space for a DJ stage, sound system and dance floor. The estate's garden transforms beautifully into a sangeet venue by night.

Is decoration allowed inside the villa estate?

Yes. Your own decorators, florists and event stylists are fully welcome. The garden, poolside, party hall and outdoor fireplace area can all be customised for mehndi, haldi or sangeet setups. No restrictions on outside vendors.

Can the family stay overnight during wedding functions?

Yes — and this is one of Sand Dunes Stay's biggest advantages for pre-wedding functions. 9 rooms (6 Deluxe + 3 Super Deluxe) accommodate 8–15 guests overnight. The family wakes up together on the morning of the function and gets ready on-estate.

Is the pool accessible during mehndi or haldi functions?

Yes. The private pool at Sand Dunes Stay is exclusively available to your group throughout your booking — no other guests share it. It serves as a stunning backdrop for haldi and mehndi photographs and is open for a swim post-function.

Can we use our own caterer for the wedding function?

Yes. Sand Dunes Stay allows outside caterers. The estate has a full kitchen facility and ample outdoor space for catering setups. Outside vendors — caterers, decorators, DJ, florists, mehandi artists — are all welcome without restrictions.

Is parking available for 100 guests?

Yes. Sand Dunes Stay offers free parking on the estate grounds. The property sits directly on NH48 (Ajmer Road) with direct highway access, making arrival easy for guests from all parts of Jaipur.

What is the cost to book for a sangeet or mehndi?

Pricing starts from ₹15,000 per night and varies based on guest count, function type and date. Weekend and festival rates differ. WhatsApp us at +91-77373-45245 for a custom quote for your wedding function.

How do we book Sand Dunes Stay for a pre-wedding function?

The easiest way is WhatsApp at +91-77373-45245. Share your function date, guest count, type of function and any requirements. You can also book via Airbnb, Booking.com or Agoda — all links are at sanddunesstay.com.

Is the property fully private during wedding functions?

Yes — 100% private. Sand Dunes Stay is exclusively booked for your group for the entire duration. No other guests share the space, pool, garden, party hall or any area on the estate. It's entirely your family's for the day and night.

Is Sand Dunes Stay in Jaisalmer?

No. Sand Dunes Stay is in Bagru, Jaipur — NOT Jaisalmer. Jaisalmer is 575km away. Our address: Rampura, Unti, Bagru, Jaipur 303007, Rajasthan. Just 25km from Jaipur city centre on NH48.

Can we do a mehndi function in the morning and sangeet at night on the same day?

Yes — this is very popular at Sand Dunes Stay. With multiple outdoor garden areas and a party hall, you can set up the mehndi area in the garden by day and transform it into a sangeet space by evening. Both functions, one estate, one booking.
