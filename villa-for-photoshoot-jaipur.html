Equipment — No Restrictions

Bring any equipment you need. Heavy lighting rigs, C-stands, generators, camera cranes — there are no restrictions. Equipment vans and large vehicles can park directly on the estate.

Changing & Makeup Spaces

Free high-speed WiFi covers the entire estate for on-set file transfer and remote client viewing. AC is available in all rooms for equipment cooling. CCTV coverage across the property for production security.
