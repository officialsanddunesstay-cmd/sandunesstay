Natural Attractions Near Sand Dunes Stay

Use Sand Dunes Stay as your base — every major natural landmark near Jaipur is within easy reach.

🌊

Chandlai Lake

25km · ~30 min

A serene seasonal lake popular for picnics, birdwatching, and photography. Best visited October–March for migratory birds.

🏞️

Ramgarh Lake

30km · ~35 min

A historic reservoir near Jaipur, scenic and peaceful — once the venue for rowing at the 1982 Asian Games.

🦩

Sambhar Lake

60km · ~60 min

India's largest inland saltwater lake — a Ramsar wetland site famous for flamingos and migratory birds. A day trip worth planning.

⛰️

Aravalli Hills

Surrounding area

The ancient Aravalli range forms the natural backdrop of Bagru and the surrounding region — gentle slopes, open vistas, and quiet trails.

🐦

Birdwatching Country

5–25km radius

The Bagru–Chandlai belt is rich in winter migratory birds — ducks, cranes, lapwings, and more — especially October to February.

Open Rajasthan Countryside

At your doorstep

The land around Sand Dunes Stay is open, quiet Rajasthan countryside — fields, open skies, and a genuine sense of space rare so close to a city.
